Decode

Decodes a recording of RADE off air — from your rig, FreeDV, or a web SDR — without touching the radio.

Transmit

Encodes a speech recording off air, for checking without a microphone. A saved file is 8 kHz mono at RADE's standard level, so it decodes in FreeDV, rade_rx_wav, or the Decode button above.

What this is. FreeDV's C port of RADE (rade_c, BSD), the Opus feature extractor and the FARGAN vocoder (BSD), compiled to WebAssembly and run entirely in this page — nothing is sent anywhere. RADE V1 only. End-of-over frames are sent and detected, but carry no callsign: FreeDV encodes callsigns with its own text scheme, which this page does not yet implement. Chrome or Edge is needed for choosing audio devices and for CAT.
